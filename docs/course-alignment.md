# Course Alignment

## Aligning Courses, Competencies, and Professional Preparation

The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework is designed to ensure that every course contributes meaningfully to one or more curriculum pillars while supporting the program's broader learning outcomes.

Rather than treating ethics, technology, environmental justice, leadership, and innovation as isolated topics, these themes are intentionally integrated throughout the curriculum.

The result is a developmental curriculum that progressively prepares students to understand justice systems, evaluate ethical dilemmas, analyze emerging technologies, address social and environmental challenges, and design innovative solutions.

## Purpose

The framework addresses a central question:

How should criminal justice education prepare students for a profession increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence, environmental challenges, technological change, ethical complexity, and evolving understandings of human agency?

Purpose

Contemporary criminal justice programs are increasingly challenged to prepare students for a profession shaped by rapid technological change, artificial intelligence, environmental pressures, evolving ethical dilemmas, and heightened public expectations of accountability. While these issues are often addressed as separate subjects, few curriculum models provide a coherent framework for integrating them across a criminal justice degree. The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework addresses this gap by offering a structured approach that connects justice, ethics, technology, environmental responsibility, and human agency as core dimensions of twenty-first-century criminal justice education.

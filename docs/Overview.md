# Overview

## Why This Framework?

Traditional criminal justice curricula were largely developed for the twentieth century.

Today's professionals must understand:

- Artificial intelligence
- Digital evidence
- Algorithmic decision-making
- Cyber-enabled crime
- Data governance
- Environmental justice
- Community engagement
- Human-centered innovation

This framework intentionally integrates these competencies throughout the curriculum rather than isolating them within specialized elective courses.

## Guiding Principles

The curriculum is built around the belief that graduates should be able to:

- Understand justice systems
- Evaluate ethical dilemmas
- Analyze power and inequality
- Critically assess technology
- Navigate AI governance
- Design human-centered solutions
- Promote environmental and community well-being

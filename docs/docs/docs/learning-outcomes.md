# Learning Outcomes

Graduates who complete curriculum informed by this framework will be able to:

## PLO 1
Explain and evaluate criminal justice institutions and processes.

## PLO 2
Apply ethical reasoning to complex justice-related situations.

## PLO 3
Analyze crime, victimization, and justice outcomes using interdisciplinary frameworks.

## PLO 4
Evaluate the impacts of technology and artificial intelligence on justice systems.

## PLO 5
Conduct ethical assessments of AI systems used in public-sector environments.

## PLO 6
Develop evidence-based policy recommendations.

## PLO 7
Design innovative and human-centered solutions to justice challenges.

## PLO 8
Communicate effectively through scholarly, professional, and public-facing formats.

## PLO 9
Evaluate environmental and community justice issues affecting vulnerable populations.

## PLO 10
Demonstrate professional responsibility, leadership, and commitment to human dignity in justice-related contexts.

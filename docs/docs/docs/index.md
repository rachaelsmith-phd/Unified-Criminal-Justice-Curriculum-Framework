## Justice, Ethics, Technology, Environment, and Human Agency in the AI Era

### Developed by Rachael B. Smith

**Criminologist • Instructional Designer • Adult Educator**

---

## Welcome

The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework is an interdisciplinary curriculum model designed to prepare future justice professionals for ethical leadership within increasingly complex technological, social, and environmental systems.

The framework integrates criminal justice, ethics, artificial intelligence, environmental justice, human-centered design, community engagement, and professional responsibility into a coherent curricular structure intended to prepare graduates for the challenges of the twenty-first century.

---

## Core Question

> How do we prepare justice professionals to govern technological systems while preserving human dignity?

# Development Roadmap

## Building the Future of Criminal Justice Education

The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework is designed as a living curriculum initiative that will continue to evolve through scholarship, instructional design, practitioner feedback, and community engagement.

The framework is currently in active development and will expand through multiple stages of implementation, assessment, and refinement.

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of this project is to contribute to the evolution of criminal justice education by integrating ethics, technology, environmental justice, human-centered design, and professional identity development into a unified curricular model.

The framework seeks to prepare future professionals who are capable of leading ethically, innovating responsibly, and responding thoughtfully to the social, technological, and environmental challenges that increasingly shape justice systems.

# Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework

Justice, Ethics, Technology, Environment, and Human Agency in the AI Era

The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework is a curriculum-development initiative designed to support the integration of ethical reasoning, technological literacy, environmental awareness, and human agency across criminal justice education.

Developed by Rachael B. Smith, Ph.D. Candidate in Curriculum & Instruction, the framework provides a conceptual structure for preparing criminal justice students to navigate emerging challenges associated with artificial intelligence, digital transformation, environmental sustainability, public service, and justice systems.

## Framework Components

- Curriculum Philosophy
- Seven Curriculum Pillars
- Course Alignment Model
- Technology & AI Integration
- Environmental Justice Integration
- Professional Ethics & Decision-Making
- Human Agency and Public Service

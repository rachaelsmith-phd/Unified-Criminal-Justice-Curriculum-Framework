# Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework

## Justice, Ethics, Technology, and Human Agency in the AI Era

Developed by Rachael B. Smith

Criminologist • Instructional Designer • Adult Educator

---

### Purpose

The Unified Criminal Justice Curriculum Framework proposes an interdisciplinary criminal justice curriculum integrating ethics, artificial intelligence, environmental justice, community engagement, human-centered design, and professional responsibility.

The framework is designed to prepare future criminal justice professionals, educators, policymakers, advocates, and researchers to navigate increasingly complex technological and social environments while preserving human dignity and promoting justice.

### Current Development Areas

- Justice Systems Literacy
- Ethics and Professional Responsibility
- Power, Inequality, and Human Experience
- Technology, AI, and Society
- Ethical AI Governance
- Justice Innovation and Human-Centered Design
- Environmental, Community, and Future Justice
